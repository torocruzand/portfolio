Delete Account — Stoic Focus

How to delete your account from the app

  1. Open Stoic Focus on your Android device.
  2. Go to the Profile screen (profile icon in the bottom bar).
  3. Scroll down to find the red "Delete Account" button.
  4. Confirm the deletion in the dialog that appears.
  5. Your account and all your data will be permanently deleted.

Data that is deleted

Data type Action
Firebase Authentication account Permanently deleted
Name and email address Permanently deleted
Tasks and focus sessions Permanently deleted
Usage statistics Permanently deleted
Profile photo (stored locally) Deleted from device
Preferences and settings Deleted from device
Important: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. No data is retained after deletion.

Deletion timeline

All data is deleted immediately upon confirming account deletion. There is no retention period.
