Stoic Focus - Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Stoic Focus ("we", "our", "the app") is a minimalist Pomodoro timer with Stoic philosophy quotes, developed by Torocruzand. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Android application.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — used for authentication and password recovery
  • Display name — shown in your profile
  • Google account info — if you sign in with Google (name, email, profile photo URL)

Usage Data

We store your app usage data to provide statistics and track your progress:

  • Focus session count and total focus time
  • Daily streak information
  • Task names, estimated times, and time spent on each task

Profile Photos

Profile photos selected from your gallery are stored locally on your device only. They are not uploaded to any server. Gravatar photos are fetched from Gravatar's service based on your email hash.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and provide personalized features
  • To save and display your focus statistics and task progress
  • To display relevant advertisements through Google AdMob
  • To send password reset emails when requested

4. Data Storage

Your account and usage data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore). Data is associated with your user account and protected by Firebase security rules that ensure only you can access your own data.

Profile photos are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to any external service.

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication — for user sign-in and account management
  • Cloud Firestore — for storing user data and statistics
  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements
  • RevenueCat — for managing premium subscriptions (coming soon)
  • Gravatar — for optional profile photo retrieval

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties, except as required by the third-party services listed above to provide app functionality.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below. Upon account deletion, all your data will be permanently removed from our servers.

8. Children's Privacy

Stoic Focus is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of your data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app and on this page.
